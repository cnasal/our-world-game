# Our World plan

## First playable version — implemented

- Phaser 2D town with touch navigation, keyboard movement, collision-aware paths, and emotes.
- React menus and responsive desktop, tablet, and phone layouts.
- Clerk login and explicit Convex world memberships.
- Editable placeholder names and colors.
- One starter home per character; visits and quick travel.
- Bank balance, transaction history, drink inventory, and consumption.
- Parcel delivery from Little Post to Cloud Café for 15 coins.
- Persistent multi-world data, room-scoped movement, reconnect status, and presence expiry.
- Isolated local preview and automated privacy/economy checks.

Before the first family session: enable Clerk’s Convex integration, add the family accounts, sign in on real devices, and choose frontend hosting. The owner account and development backend are already configured. Real ten-device concurrency, physical iPad Safari behavior, and actual sign-in remain family acceptance checks.

## Next: make playing together useful

- Café shifts: one player takes an order, another prepares it, another buys it.
- Computer-controlled orders and shopkeepers whenever family members are absent.
- Gifts and a mailbox for asynchronous play.
- Furniture placement and a few things to buy for home.
- Bigger picture-led instructions and optional read-aloud prompts.
- Tune movement traffic after real multi-device playtests; add a dedicated movement service only if measured performance or usage warrants it.

## Then: make it ours

- Child-created books, toys, drinks, and shops.
- Browser Create mode with drafts, previews, and adult publishing.
- Shared projects such as a playground or garden.
- More jobs and co-op activities.
- Home visit permissions, decoration permissions, and a world-management UI.

## Design agreements

Cozy 2D visuals. Private by default. No four-player assumptions. Ten simultaneous players is the initial testing target. No rent, debt, mandatory hunger, timers, or missed-day penalties. Solo play must remain worthwhile. Start small, play together, and expand what the family enjoys.
